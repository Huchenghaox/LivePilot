# Code of Conduct

LivePilot welcomes contributions from people building safer and more useful AI-assisted live-stream operations tools.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and product outcomes.
- Assume good intent, but be clear when something creates risk.
- Avoid sharing private user data, secrets, or sensitive screenshots.
- Keep discussions welcoming to non-native English and Chinese speakers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, tokens, passwords, private screenshots, or exploit details in public threads.
- Promoting platform abuse, scraping private systems, Cookie import, simulated login, or moderation bypass techniques.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or block users when behavior harms the project or community.

Security issues should be reported privately according to `SECURITY.md`.
